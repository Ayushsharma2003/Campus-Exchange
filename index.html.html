About Campus Exchange

This is a simple marketplace built for college students. Seniors who are done using things like calculators, drafters, lab coats, cycles or coolers can list them here instead of letting them gather dust — and juniors who need those exact things can find them cheap, right on campus.

  • 📤 List it — post your item with a price and a short description.
  • 💰 You set the price — it's entirely up to you, and it's negotiable if you want.
  • 💬 Direct contact — buyers reach you straight away on WhatsApp or email. No middleman, no commission.
